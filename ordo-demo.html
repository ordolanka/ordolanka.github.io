New beneficial ownership rules apply to every company

Since 30 March 2026, Sri Lankan companies must record and report the people who ultimately own or control them, and report changes within set deadlines. Penalties for missing them are heavy. We can check where your company stands.

How it works

Starting a new company or moving from another secretary, the steps are the same.

  1. Talk to us

    A free call about your company, what is already filed and what is due. You get a fixed quote.

Questions we hear often

Can you take over from our current company secretary?

Yes. We ask your current secretary for the registers and records, check what has been filed, and fix anything that is missing. The change of secretary is filed with the Registrar as part of the handover.

Do I need to visit your office?

No. Documents are shared, approved and signed through the client portal. We are happy to meet in person if you prefer.

What is a beneficial owner?

The person who ultimately owns or controls a company, even through other companies or nominees. The new rules set the line at 10% of shares or voting rights, or control by other means. We work out who counts for your company.

Some of our directors live abroad. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Directors can review and sign online from anywhere. We tell you if any filing needs an original signature or a person resident in Sri Lanka.

How do you keep our information safe?

Every login uses two-step sign-in, records are stored encrypted, and each client only sees their own companies. We handle personal data under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act.
